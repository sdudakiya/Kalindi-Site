AEO Services FAQs

Do all pages need FAQ schema?

No. FAQ schema should only be used when visible FAQs exist on the page. Kalindi prioritizes pages where questions match real search intent.

What content formats work best for AEO?

Definitions, short answers, tables, numbered steps, checklists, pros and cons, comparison grids and evidence-backed FAQs tend to work well.

How quickly can AEO work?

Some snippet and PAA improvements can appear after recrawling, but reliable gains usually require 4 to 12 weeks of content, internal linking and authority improvements.
