2. **Cost/pricing pages** — seoserviceinindia.co.in ranks for pricing. We need "How Much Does SEO Cost in India" and "How Much Does Digital Marketing Cost in India."
3. **Comparison pages** — techeasify ranks with comparison articles. We need "SEO Agency vs In-House," "Best SEO Tools Compared," etc.
4. **GEO listicle with survey data** — getcito.com ranks #3 with original survey data. We should create something similar but better/deeper.
5. **Industry-specific landing pages** — None of the competitors have pages like "SEO for E-Commerce India" or "SEO for SaaS India." We should create these.

### Medium Priority:
6. **SEO audit tool / free tool** — A free SEO audit tool generates backlinks and leads
7. **Templates/checklists** — Downloadable resources attract links
8. **Video content** — YouTube results appear in SERP (TheMediaAnt, upGrowth, Softtrix)
9. **FAQ/PAA targeting** — AI Overview citations come from well-structured FAQ content
