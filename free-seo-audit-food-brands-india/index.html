What is GEO and why should I care?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of optimizing your brand to be cited by AI engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude. When a consumer asks "What's the best healthy snack brand in India?" or "Which restaurant in Pune has the best pani puri?", AI generates an answer from the sources it trusts. If your brand isn't one of those sources, you're invisible to an audience that's growing by millions every month. Our audit includes a full GEO readiness assessment.
