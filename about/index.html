Our Story

From a Simple Idea to India's AI-Era Marketing Agency

Kalindi Marketing was born from a simple observation: digital marketing agencies were stuck in the past. Most were still running the same playbooks from 2015 — keyword stuffing, generic backlinks, and vanity metrics — while the search landscape was transforming beneath their feet.

We were founded with a single mission: to build an agency that not only masters traditional SEO, but truly understands and optimizes for the AI-powered future of search. That means Answer Engine Optimization, Generative Engine Optimization, and everything in between.

Today, we're proud to serve 100+ clients across India and globally, helping businesses of all sizes become the most visible, most trusted, and most cited brands in their industries — across Google, ChatGPT, Gemini, and beyond.

How We Work

The Kalindi Search Operating System

Every engagement follows the same disciplined loop: diagnose the market, build the authority base, publish useful assets, measure what changes, and improve the next sprint.

Discovery & Market Mapping

We start by mapping search demand, AI answer surfaces, competitor content, technical blockers, and conversion gaps. The goal is to know which topics can drive revenue, not just traffic.

  • Keyword clusters by buyer intent
  • Google, Maps, AI Overview, and LLM visibility checks
  • Technical crawl, content quality, and internal-link review

Execution Sprints

We turn strategy into weekly shipping: landing pages, content briefs, schema, FAQs, conversion copy, technical fixes, and link-worthy assets built around clear priorities.

  • SEO, AEO, and GEO improvements shipped together
  • Content refreshes for pages with ranking potential
  • Measurement snapshots so every sprint has a baseline

SEO Technical health, topical authority, content depth, and internal links More qualified impressions, rankings, and organic leads
