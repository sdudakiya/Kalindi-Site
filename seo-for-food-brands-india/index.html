Why Most Food Brands Are Invisible to Modern Search

The Indian food and beverage market is worth over ₹50,000 crore and growing at 12% annually. But here's the problem: 90% of food brands are invisible where it matters most — on Google's first page, in AI Overviews, and in the answers generated by ChatGPT and Gemini.

Consider these realities:

  • Google's first page captures 95% of clicks. If your food brand isn't on Page 1 for your target keywords, you don't exist for most potential customers.
  • AI Overviews now appear in 47% of search results. Google's AI-generated answers sit above traditional results, pushing organic listings further down the page. If your content isn't optimized for AEO, you're losing visibility even when you rank.
  • ChatGPT handles 200 million queries daily. When someone asks "Which is the best A2 ghee brand in India?" or "What are the top D2C food brands?", the AI generates an answer from the sources it trusts. If your brand isn't one of them, you're missing an entirely new discovery channel.
  • Local search drives 76% of restaurant visits. For restaurant chains, Google Maps ranking is make-or-break. A #1 ranking vs. a #10 ranking can mean the difference between 3,500+ visitors and near-zero footfall.
  • Food content is ultra-competitive. Keywords like "best protein bar India" or "A2 ghee benefits" have domain authority requirements that generic content can't meet. You need E-E-A-T signals, structured data, and topical authority.
