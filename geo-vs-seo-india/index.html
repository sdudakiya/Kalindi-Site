1. What is SEO?

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the practice of improving your website's visibility in organic (non-paid) search engine results on platforms like Google, Bing, and Yahoo. When someone searches for a keyword related to your business, SEO determines whether your website appears — and how high it ranks.

SEO has been the foundation of digital marketing for over two decades. It encompasses:

  • Technical SEO: Site speed, mobile-friendliness, crawlability, structured data, and site architecture
  • On-Page SEO: Keyword optimization, meta tags, heading structure, internal linking, and content quality
  • Off-Page SEO: Backlink building, brand mentions, social signals, and domain authority development
  • Local SEO: Google Business Profile optimization, local citations, reviews, and map pack rankings

For Indian businesses, SEO remains the primary driver of organic website traffic. With over 900 million internet users in India and Google commanding 95%+ search market share, ranking on Google is still the single most impactful digital marketing activity.

2. What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of optimizing your brand's digital presence to be cited, recommended, and referenced by AI-powered generative engines — including ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and Microsoft Copilot.

While SEO focuses on ranking in a list of search results, GEO focuses on a fundamentally different question: "When an AI engine generates an answer, does it mention your brand?"

This distinction matters because AI engines don't return a list of links. They generate a single, synthesized answer. If your brand isn't the source that AI draws from, you're invisible to everyone who uses that engine — and that audience is growing explosively in India.

GEO encompasses:

  • Entity Authority: Establishing your brand as a well-defined entity across the web (Wikipedia, Wikidata, Knowledge Graph, news mentions)
  • Citation Density: Building mentions across authoritative Indian publications, blogs, directories, and review sites
  • E-E-A-T Signals: Demonstrating Expertise, Experience, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness — amplified for AI consumption
  • AI-Citable Content: Creating well-structured, factual content with clear headings, data points, and direct answers
  • Conversational Query Optimization: Targeting the exact questions users ask AI engines, not just typed keywords
📈 GEO is not a replacement for SEO — it's the next layer. The most future-proof strategy combines both: rank on Google AND get cited by AI. Less than 5% of Indian businesses have any GEO strategy, making this the single biggest competitive opportunity in Indian digital marketing right now.

7. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between GEO and SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) focuses on ranking your website in traditional search engine results pages (SERPs) like Google and Bing. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) focuses on getting your brand cited, recommended, and referenced by AI-powered generative engines like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude. SEO targets keyword rankings; GEO targets AI citations and brand mentions in generated answers.

Should Indian businesses invest in GEO or SEO in 2026?

Indian businesses should invest in both SEO and GEO. SEO remains essential — Google still drives the majority of web traffic. But GEO is rapidly growing in importance as over 95 million Indians now use ChatGPT monthly and AI Overviews appear in ~40% of Google searches. The most effective strategy combines both: rank on Google AND get cited by AI engines. Businesses that focus only on SEO risk becoming invisible to the growing AI search audience.

How long does it take to see results from GEO?

GEO results typically take 4-12 weeks to become measurable, depending on your brand's existing authority and the competitiveness of your industry. Early results may include appearing in AI responses for long-tail queries within 2-4 weeks. Building strong entity authority and citation density — the foundations of GEO — takes 2-3 months of consistent effort. In the Parampara.farm case study, the brand began appearing in ChatGPT and Gemini responses within 30 days of implementing GEO strategy.

Is GEO more expensive than SEO for Indian businesses?

GEO is not necessarily more expensive than SEO, but it requires different investments. Traditional SEO costs include technical optimization, link building, and content creation. GEO adds costs for entity authority building, citation management, AI-content optimization, and AI citation monitoring. However, many GEO activities (like creating authoritative, well-structured content and building brand mentions) overlap with SEO. A combined SEO+GEO strategy from Kalindi Marketing is designed to be cost-efficient by leveraging shared activities.

Which Indian industries benefit most from GEO?

The Indian industries that benefit most from GEO include: E-Commerce and D2C brands (product recommendations are the #1 AI search use case), SaaS and Technology (buyers compare tools via AI before purchasing), Healthcare and Wellness (patients ask AI for provider recommendations), Education and EdTech (students use AI to compare institutions), Real Estate (buyers ask AI for locality and builder recommendations), and Food & Beverage (restaurant and food recommendations via AI). Any industry where consumers make purchase decisions based on recommendations can benefit significantly from GEO.
