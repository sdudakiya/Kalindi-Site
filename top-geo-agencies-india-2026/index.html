📊 Comparison Framework: How to Evaluate a GEO Agency

Not all GEO agencies are created equal. Here's the framework we used — and what you should look for when evaluating partners:

Criteria What Good Looks Like Red Flags
AI Citation Tracking Proprietary dashboard tracking brand mentions across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude ✗ No measurement tools, only promises
Methodology Documented GEO framework with clear stages: audit → optimize → measure → iterate ✗ "We do SEO but for AI" — no distinct methodology
Multi-Platform Covers ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overviews ~ Only focuses on one platform
Case Studies Verified results with specific metrics (citation rates, traffic growth, AI mention share) ✗ Vague claims without numbers
E-E-A-T Focus Builds author credentials, structured data, brand authority signals ✗ No mention of expertise/authority building
Content Strategy Creates AI-citable content: structured answers, data-driven insights, expert quotes ~ Only does keyword stuffing for AI
Reporting Monthly AI visibility reports with citation tracking, sentiment, share of voice ✗ Only provides traditional SEO reports
Pricing Transparent pricing with clear scope of work and deliverables ✗ Hidden fees, vague scope

Key Questions to Ask Any GEO Agency

  1. How do you measure AI citations? — They should show you a live dashboard, not just a PDF.
  2. Which AI platforms do you optimize for? — At minimum: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity.
  3. Can you show me a case study with verified results? — Look for specific metrics, not just testimonials.
  4. How is GEO different from your SEO service? — If they can't articulate the difference, walk away.
  5. What's your content strategy for AI visibility? — Should include structured data, E-E-A-T signals, and AI-citable formats.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

A GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) agency specializes in optimizing your brand's content and online presence to be cited, referenced, and recommended by AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude. Unlike traditional SEO agencies that focus on Google rankings, GEO agencies focus on building the structured authority signals that AI models use when generating answers for users. This includes creating AI-citable content, implementing structured data, building brand authority through E-E-A-T signals, and tracking how often your brand appears in AI responses.

GEO agency pricing in India ranges from ₹30,000/month for basic AI visibility audits to ₹2,00,000+/month for comprehensive GEO programs covering content optimization, structured data, brand authority building, and multi-platform AI monitoring. Most mid-tier agencies charge between ₹75,000–₹1,50,000/month depending on scope and company size. Kalindi Marketing starts at ₹75,000/month with a free initial audit to determine the right scope for your business.

Look for agencies that provide AI mention tracking reports showing how often your brand appears in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity responses for target queries. Key metrics include: AI citation rate (how often AI mentions your brand), share of voice in AI responses, branded query visibility in AI platforms, and sentiment of AI mentions. Any credible GEO agency should show you a dashboard with these metrics — not just traditional SEO reports. Ask for a live demo of their tracking tools before signing.
