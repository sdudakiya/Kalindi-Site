Trend 1: AI Overviews & SGE — The New Search Reality

Google's AI Overviews (formerly SGE) now appear in approximately 40% of Indian search queries in 2026. This single change has fundamentally altered how businesses need to think about search visibility.

📊 The Data: AI Overviews capture 60-70% of the SERP "above the fold" real estate. Organic results below the fold receive 25-40% fewer clicks when an AI Overview is present.

What to do: Structure content to be cited by AI Overviews. Write clear, factual, well-sourced answers. Use data and statistics. Include FAQ sections. Optimize for "position zero" — the content that AI Overview pulls from.

Trend 2: GEO — Generative Engine Optimization

While SEO optimizes for Google's 10 blue links, GEO optimizes for AI engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude that generate answers instead of lists.

📊 The Data: ChatGPT has 95M+ monthly active users in India. 67% of Indian internet users aged 18-35 have tried at least one AI search tool. Less than 5% of Indian businesses have a GEO strategy.

What to do: Establish entity authority. Build citation density across authoritative sources. Create AI-citable content with clear, factual statements. Monitor AI citations regularly. See our State of GEO in India 2026 report for the complete strategy.

Trend 3: E-E-A-T as a Ranking Factor

Google has elevated E-E-A-T (Expertise, Experience, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) from a guideline to an active ranking signal. This is especially impactful for YMYL (Your Money Your Life) topics but increasingly affects all categories.

📊 The Data: Google's 2025-2026 algorithm updates specifically target content lacking demonstrable expertise. Sites without clear author credentials and real-world experience signals have seen 15-30% traffic declines.

What to do: Add author bios with credentials. Showcase real client results with metrics. Get mentioned in authoritative publications. Include original research and data. Demonstrate hands-on experience in content.

Frequently Asked Questions

The biggest trend is the rise of AI Overviews and GEO. Google's AI-generated answers now appear in ~40% of Indian searches, fundamentally changing how businesses need to optimize.

Is GEO replacing traditional SEO in India?

No. GEO complements SEO. The most effective strategy optimizes for both traditional search rankings AND AI citations.
