Quick answer: LLM Optimization improves the signals large language models use to understand your brand: consistent entity facts, structured pages, expert content, accessible crawling, third-party mentions and clear evidence for claims.

What LLM Optimization Solves

Large language models can summarize a business incorrectly when the web contains thin content, conflicting descriptions, missing schema, unclear service pages or weak third-party evidence. LLM Optimization reduces that ambiguity by making your brand easier to understand and verify.

The Signals We Improve

Entity signals

Company description, service taxonomy, location, audience, case studies, founders, social profiles and knowledge graph clues.

Content signals

Definitions, answer blocks, FAQs, tables, methodology notes, proof points and source-backed claims.

Authority signals

Relevant mentions, directories, reviews, case studies, original data and links from credible industry sources.

LLM Optimization FAQs

Do LLMs read llms.txt?

`llms.txt` is an emerging convention, not a guaranteed ranking factor. Kalindi uses it as one clarity signal alongside sitemap, robots.txt, schema, internal links and accessible HTML.

What is the first page to optimize?

Start with the homepage, services hub, highest-value service pages, case studies and any report or guide you want AI systems to cite.

Does this help SEO too?

Yes. Clear entities, structured answers, stronger internal links and credible proof also support traditional SEO and AEO performance.
