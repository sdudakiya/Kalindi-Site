AI Visibility FAQs

Can AI visibility be tracked perfectly?

No. AI answers can vary by prompt wording, location, account state and retrieval behavior. The goal is directional measurement across a consistent prompt set.

What if AI describes my brand incorrectly?

We identify the likely source of confusion, then correct your owned pages, schema, profiles and external descriptions so better evidence is available.

Which team should own AI visibility?

SEO, content, PR and brand teams should share ownership. AI visibility is influenced by technical access, content quality, third-party mentions and entity consistency.
