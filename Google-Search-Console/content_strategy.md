## 🏗️ Content Structure Best Practices

For every article, follow this template to maximize SEO + AEO + GEO performance:

1. **Title**: Include the primary keyword naturally
2. **Meta Description**: 150-160 chars, compelling, includes keyword
3. **H1**: Match the title tag
4. **Introduction**: Answer the core question in the first 100 words *(critical for AEO)*
5. **Table of Contents**: Add jump links for longer posts
6. **Subheadings (H2/H3)**: Use question-based headings *(e.g., "What is AEO?")*
7. **FAQ Section**: Add 3-5 FAQs with concise answers using `FAQPage` schema markup
8. **Internal Links**: Link to your service pages and related blog posts
9. **Author Bio**: Add E-E-A-T signals — author name, credentials, photo
10. **Schema Markup**: `Article`, `FAQPage`, and `Organization` schema on every post
