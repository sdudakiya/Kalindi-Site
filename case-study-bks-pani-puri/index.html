Case Study: BK's Pani Puri Gallery

🚀 From Stall to Scale: How we drove 3,500+ real-world visitors in exactly 3 days.

The Challenge

Breaking into the crowded local street food market is no easy feat. BK's Pani Puri Gallery needed a way to stand out instantly and drive massive foot traffic from day one. Relying purely on traditional walk-ins wasn't enough; they needed a buzz that would echo across the entire city.

The Strategy: A Digital-First Launch

At Kalindi Marketing, we completely reversed the traditional restaurant launch model. Instead of opening the doors and hoping people would show up, we engineered a comprehensive Digital-First Launch Strategy before they even launched.

  • Hyper-Local SEO: We optimized their local map listings and business profiles to dominate "near me" searches in their geographical radius.
  • Viral Content Engineering: We designed and executed highly shareable, engaging social content leading up to the launch day to build anticipation.
  • Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): We ensured AI-driven search engines and conversational AI models were accurately recommending BK's Pani Puri Gallery for local food queries.

🔥 35,000+ Organic Impressions across local search and social platforms

🚶‍♂️ 3,500+ Real-World Visitors in a single launch weekend

The Results

The digital-first approach paid off exponentially. The organic push generated immediate market authority in the local street food sector. Not only did they see thousands of visitors over the launch weekend, but they established a foundational digital presence that continues to drive sustained growth month over month.
