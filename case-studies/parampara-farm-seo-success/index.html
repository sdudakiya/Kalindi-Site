About Parampara.farm

Parampara.farm is a premium D2C food brand specializing in A2 ghee, hing (asafoetida), and saffron — traditional Indian ingredients sourced directly from farms. The brand positions itself at the intersection of authenticity, quality, and modern convenience, targeting health-conscious Indian consumers who want traditional ingredients without compromise.

When Parampara.farm approached Kalindi Marketing, they were a relatively new brand with strong products but minimal digital presence. Their website had been live for less than 3 months, domain authority was near zero, and they were competing against established players like Amul, Patanjali, and Heritage Farms for high-value food keywords.

The Challenge

Parampara.farm faced the classic D2C food brand dilemma: excellent product, passionate founders, but invisible online. Specifically:

  • Zero domain authority: New website with no backlink profile, making it nearly impossible to rank for competitive food keywords
  • Ultra-competitive keywords: Target keywords like "buy A2 ghee online" and "best hing brand" were dominated by established brands with years of SEO investment
  • E-E-A-T deficit: Google's quality guidelines for food/health content (YMYL) require demonstrable expertise — something new brands struggle to prove
  • No local presence: As a D2C brand, they needed to build trust signals that physical retailers naturally have
  • Limited content: Product pages only, no blog, no authority content, no thought leadership

Our Strategy

We designed a 30-day intensive SEO sprint followed by a sustained growth program. Here's exactly what we did:

Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-7)

  • Technical SEO audit: Fixed crawl errors, improved site speed from 3.2s to 1.1s, implemented proper heading hierarchy, added structured data (Product, Organization, BreadcrumbList schemas)
  • Keyword mapping: Identified 47 target keywords across 3 categories: buyer-intent ("buy A2 ghee online India"), informational ("A2 ghee vs regular ghee"), and brand-protection ("Parampara ghee review")
  • On-page optimization: Rewrote all product titles, meta descriptions, and H1 tags. Added FAQ sections to product pages targeting long-tail queries
  • Content calendar: Planned 12 blog posts targeting informational keywords that drive top-of-funnel traffic

Phase 2: Authority Building (Days 8-21)

  • E-E-A-T content creation: Published 6 in-depth blog posts authored by a food industry expert, covering topics like "The Complete Guide to A2 Ghee: Benefits, Taste, and How to Identify Authentic Products" and "Hing in Indian Cooking: Why Asafoetida is the Secret Ingredient of Every Indian Kitchen"
  • Digital PR: Secured mentions in 8 food and wellness publications, including features on "Top 10 D2C Food Brands to Watch in India" lists
  • Backlink acquisition: Built 23 quality backlinks from food blogs, health publications, and industry directories through guest posts, product reviews, and expert commentary
  • Social proof integration: Added customer review schema, trust badges, and media mention logos to the website

Phase 3: AEO + GEO Optimization (Days 22-30)

  • Featured snippet optimization: Restructured content to target position-zero snippets for queries like "what is A2 ghee" and "benefits of hing"
  • AI citation signals: Created authoritative, citable content formats (listicles, comparison guides, expert roundups) that AI engines prefer to cite
  • Entity markup: Implemented Organization and Product schema to help AI engines recognize Parampara.farm as a distinct brand entity
  • llms.txt creation: Added structured brand information to help AI crawlers understand the brand's authority in the FMCG food space

Key Takeaways for D2C Food Brands

This case study demonstrates several principles that apply to any D2C food brand looking to grow through SEO:

  1. Technical SEO is non-negotiable: No amount of great content will rank on a slow, poorly structured website. Fix the foundation first.
  2. E-E-A-T is the differentiator: In food and health, Google rewards brands that demonstrate real expertise. Invest in expert-authored content.
  3. Speed comes from focus: We targeted a specific set of keywords with a concentrated 30-day effort rather than spreading thin across hundreds of terms.
  4. Digital PR accelerates everything: Media mentions and backlinks from food publications built authority faster than any other tactic.
  5. AEO + GEO is the future: Optimizing for AI Overviews and AI engine citations gave Parampara.farm visibility channels that competitors weren't even thinking about.
