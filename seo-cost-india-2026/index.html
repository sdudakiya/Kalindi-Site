SEO Pricing by Engagement Model

1. Monthly Retainer (Most Common)

The most popular model for SEO in India. You pay a fixed monthly fee for ongoing optimization work. Best for businesses that want consistent, long-term growth.

  • Pros: Predictable costs, continuous optimization, dedicated team
  • Cons: Requires 3-6 month commitment for meaningful results
  • Best for: Established businesses with ongoing marketing needs

2. Project-Based SEO

Pay a fixed price for a specific deliverable — like an audit, a website migration, or a content campaign. Best for one-time needs.

  • Pros: Clear scope, fixed cost, defined timeline
  • Cons: No ongoing optimization after project ends
  • Best for: New website launches, site migrations, one-time audits

3. Performance-Based SEO

Pay based on results achieved — rankings, traffic, or revenue. Rare in India because SEO results depend on many factors outside the agency's control.

  • Pros: Aligned incentives, pay for results
  • Cons: Very few reputable agencies offer this; often comes with caveats
  • Best for: Businesses with high confidence in their market

4. Hourly Consulting

Pay by the hour for expert SEO guidance. Best for businesses with in-house teams that need strategic direction.

  • Rate: ₹2,000 – ₹10,000/hour depending on expertise
  • Best for: In-house marketing teams, CMOs, founders who want to DIY
💡 Pro Tip: The cheapest SEO is almost always the most expensive. Agencies offering ₹5,000/month SEO typically skip technical optimization, use spammy link building, and produce AI-generated content that Google penalizes. Budget at least ₹25,000/month for meaningful results.

What Affects SEO Pricing in India?

Several factors determine how much you'll pay for SEO:

  • Competition level: More competitive keywords (e.g., "best CRM software") cost more to rank for than niche terms (e.g., "CRM for Indian textile manufacturers")
  • Current site health: A well-built site with clean technical foundations costs less to optimize than one with years of accumulated issues
  • Content requirements: Industries requiring expert content (healthcare, finance, legal) cost more than general business content
  • Geographic scope: National/international SEO costs more than local SEO for a single city
  • Agency experience: Agencies with proven case studies and specialized expertise (like GEO) charge more — and deliver better results

How to Evaluate SEO Pricing Quotes

When comparing SEO agencies in India, ask these questions:

  1. What's included in the monthly fee? Get a specific deliverable list, not vague promises
  2. What's your link building strategy? If they can't explain it clearly, walk away
  3. Can I see case studies with real numbers? Vague "we increased traffic" claims are meaningless
  4. What happens if I cancel? Understand the exit terms before signing
  5. Do you offer GEO/AI optimization? In 2026, this should be part of any serious SEO engagement
